Sweep bundles one third-party program in its release zip.

PresentMon.exe (console application, version 2.5.1)
  Copyright (c) Intel Corporation
  License: MIT
  Source: https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon
  Used by Sweep's Games tab "Test Run" to read frame times. It only listens to
  Windows presentation events. It never touches or injects into games.
  The Sweep app itself never connects to the internet.
